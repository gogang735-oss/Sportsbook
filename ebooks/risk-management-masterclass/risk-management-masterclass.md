## MODULE 1: WHY RISK MANAGEMENT IS THE SKILL {#module-1}

### Let Me Hit You With a Truth Bomb

I don't care how good your strategy is. I don't care if you've got the best entries on the planet. If your risk management is rubbish, you WILL blow your account. It's not a matter of "if." It's "when."

I've seen traders with 80% win rates blow accounts. How? Because when they lost, they lost BIG. One bad trade wiped out twenty good ones.

And I've seen traders with 40% win rates make consistent money month after month. How? Because their winners were 3x bigger than their losers. And they never risked more than they could afford to lose.

That's the game. Risk management isn't some boring chapter you skip. It IS trading.

### The Three Types of Traders

```
TYPE 1: THE GAMBLER
─────────────────────────────────────────
- No fixed lot size
- "Feels" like risking more when confident
- One big loss = weeks of profits gone
- Blows 3-4 accounts per year
- Thinks risk management is "boring"
- Will never make it long-term

TYPE 2: THE SCARED TRADER
─────────────────────────────────────────
- Risks so little that wins don't matter
- Takes profit too early out of fear
- Never grows the account meaningfully
- Can't handle drawdowns — quits after 3 losses
- Survives but never thrives

TYPE 3: THE PROFESSIONAL
─────────────────────────────────────────
- Fixed % risk per trade (never deviates)
- Lets winners run to full target
- Accepts losses as part of the maths
- Survives drawdowns because they planned for them
- Compounds steadily over months and years
- THIS is who you want to be
```

### The Numbers That Should Scare You

```
ACCOUNT BLOW-UP STATISTICS:
──────────────────────────────────────────

- 75% of retail traders lose money (FCA data)
- Average account lifespan: 3-6 months
- #1 reason for failure: Overleveraging / poor risk management
- #2 reason: Revenge trading after losses
- #3 reason: No stop loss
```

Every single one of those reasons is a RISK MANAGEMENT problem. Not a strategy problem. Not a knowledge problem. A discipline problem.

### What Risk Management Actually Means

It's dead simple. Risk management answers THREE questions:

```
1. HOW MUCH can I lose on this trade? (Position sizing)
2. WHERE will I exit if I'm wrong? (Stop loss placement)
3. HOW MUCH total risk am I carrying right now? (Exposure management)
```

If you can answer those three questions before EVERY trade, you're ahead of 90% of traders.

## MODULE 2: POSITION SIZING — THE MATHS THAT KEEPS YOU ALIVE {#module-2}

### The Golden Rule: Never Risk More Than 1-2% Per Trade

This isn't some arbitrary number. It's backed by decades of probability maths. Let me show you why.

### The Fixed Percentage Model

```
THE FORMULA:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Position Size (Lots) = (Account Balance × Risk %) ÷ (Stop Loss in Pips × Pip Value)

EXAMPLE:
Account: £10,000
Risk: 1% = £100
Stop Loss: 30 pips
Pip Value (GBPUSD, 1 standard lot): £7.70/pip

Position Size = £100 ÷ (30 × £7.70) = £100 ÷ £231 = 0.43 lots
```

### Why 1% Works — The Survival Maths

At 1% risk per trade, here's what happens during a losing streak:

```
CONSECUTIVE LOSSES AT 1% RISK:
──────────────────────────────────────────

Starting balance: £10,000

After 5 losses:   £9,510  (still fine)
After 10 losses:  £9,044  (uncomfortable but alive)
After 15 losses:  £8,601  (rough patch but recoverable)
After 20 losses:  £8,179  (bad month but account intact)

Now compare with 5% risk per trade:

After 5 losses:   £7,738  (23% drawdown already)
After 10 losses:  £5,987  (40% drawdown — panic mode)
After 15 losses:  £4,633  (over 50% gone — mentally broken)
After 20 losses:  £3,585  (account effectively dead)
```

See the difference? At 1%, twenty losses in a row (which is extremely unlikely with any decent strategy) still leaves you with 80% of your account. At 5%, you're basically done.

### The Recovery Problem

This is the bit most people don't think about:

```
LOSS vs. RECOVERY NEEDED:
──────────────────────────────────────────

Lost 5%   → Need 5.3% to get back to breakeven
Lost 10%  → Need 11.1% to recover
Lost 20%  → Need 25% to recover
Lost 30%  → Need 42.9% to recover
Lost 40%  → Need 66.7% to recover
Lost 50%  → Need 100% to recover (double your remaining money)
Lost 60%  → Need 150% to recover
Lost 80%  → Need 400% to recover (basically impossible)
```

The deeper you dig the hole, the harder it is to climb out. That's why PRESERVATION is the first priority. Growth comes second.

### Position Sizing Quick Reference Table

```

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Stop Loss    Lot Size    Pips to TP (1:2)    Potential Profit

15 pips      0.87 lots   30 pips             £200
20 pips      0.65 lots   40 pips             £200

40 pips      0.33 lots   80 pips             £200
50 pips      0.26 lots   100 pips            £200

NOTICE: The reward is ALWAYS £200 (1:2 RR) regardless of stop size.
The lot size adjusts to keep your risk constant at £100.
```

### When to Adjust Risk %

```
INCREASE to 1.5-2%:
- After 3+ consecutive wins (confidence is high, strategy is working)
- When your account is up 10%+ from starting balance
- On A++ setups (perfect alignment across all criteria)

DECREASE to 0.5%:
- After 2+ consecutive losses
- When in drawdown (account below starting balance)
- During news-heavy days
- When trading a new/untested setup
- When emotionally off (tired, angry, distracted)

NEVER exceed 2% per trade. NEVER.
```

### Multiple Position Management

What if you want to take 2-3 trades at the same time?

```
TOTAL EXPOSURE RULE:
──────────────────────────────────────────

Maximum total risk at any one time: 3-5% of account

Example:
- Trade 1: 1% risk on EURUSD
- Trade 2: 1% risk on GBPUSD
- Trade 3: 1% risk on USDJPY
- Total exposure: 3% ← ACCEPTABLE

BUT if all three pairs are correlated (e.g., all USD pairs moving together), 
your REAL exposure might be higher. Be careful.

CORRELATION RULE:
- Never have more than 2% risk on correlated pairs simultaneously
- EUR/USD and GBP/USD are 80%+ correlated — treat them as ONE risk
```

## MODULE 3: DRAWDOWN MANAGEMENT — SURVIVING THE BAD TIMES {#module-3}

### Everyone Has Drawdowns. It's How You Handle Them That Matters.

A drawdown is the distance between your account's peak and its current value. Every single successful trader has experienced drawdowns. The difference is they PLANNED for them.

### What's a "Normal" Drawdown?

```
ACCEPTABLE DRAWDOWN BY STRATEGY TYPE:
──────────────────────────────────────────

Scalping (high win rate):     5-10% max drawdown expected
Day Trading:                  10-15% max drawdown expected
Swing Trading:                15-20% max drawdown expected
Position Trading:             20-25% max drawdown expected

If your drawdown exceeds these ranges, something is WRONG.
Either your strategy isn't working in current conditions,
or you're deviating from your plan.
```

### The Drawdown Stages — And How to Respond

```
STAGE 1: NORMAL (0-5% drawdown)
──────────────────────────────────────────
Feeling: "Part of the game"
Action: Continue trading normally
Risk: Keep at standard 1%

STAGE 2: UNCOMFORTABLE (5-10% drawdown)
──────────────────────────────────────────
Feeling: "Something might be off"
Action: Review recent trades. Are they valid setups?
Risk: Reduce to 0.75%
Rule: Maximum 1 trade per day

STAGE 3: PAINFUL (10-15% drawdown)
──────────────────────────────────────────
Feeling: "I need to make this back"  ← DANGER
Action: STOP TRADING for 2-3 days minimum
Risk: When you return, reduce to 0.5%
Rule: Only A++ setups. Maximum 1 trade per day.
Focus: Rebuilding confidence, not recovering money

STAGE 4: CRITICAL (15-20% drawdown)
──────────────────────────────────────────
Feeling: "I'm broken"
Action: STOP TRADING for 1 full week
Risk: Full strategy review — is the market regime different?
Rule: Return to demo for 1 week before live trading
Consider: Is this a strategy failure or a discipline failure?

STAGE 5: EMERGENCY (20%+ drawdown)
──────────────────────────────────────────
Feeling: Panic / Desperation / Numbness
Action: STOP COMPLETELY
Risk: Your account AND your mental health are at stake
Rule: Do not trade live until you've been profitable on demo for 2+ weeks
Consider: Smaller account, different market, or mentor support
```

### The "Circuit Breaker" System

Professional trading firms have circuit breakers — automatic stops when losses hit certain levels. You need them too.

```
MY PERSONAL CIRCUIT BREAKERS:
──────────────────────────────────────────

Daily loss limit: 2% → Stop trading for the day
Weekly loss limit: 4% → Reduce risk to 0.5% for remaining week
Monthly loss limit: 6% → Stop live trading. Review everything.
```

Write these down. Stick them on your monitor. Make them non-negotiable.

### Drawdown Recovery Strategy

When you're in drawdown, the WORST thing you can do is try to "make it back fast." That's gambling. Here's the professional approach:

```
THE RECOVERY PROTOCOL:
──────────────────────────────────────────

1. ACCEPT the drawdown. It's done. You can't un-lose the money.
2. REDUCE risk. Don't try to recover with bigger positions.
3. QUALITY over quantity. Take fewer trades, only the best setups.
4. DOCUMENT what went wrong. Was it strategy or discipline?
5. SET a realistic recovery timeframe. If you lost 10% over 2 weeks,
   plan to recover it over 4-6 weeks (not 2 days).
6. CELEBRATE small wins. Every green day is progress.
```

### The Compounding Effect of Small, Consistent Gains

Here's what happens when you just make 1-2% per week consistently:

```
STARTING BALANCE: £10,000
WEEKLY GAIN: 1.5% (that's just 1-2 good trades per week)
──────────────────────────────────────────

After 1 month:   £10,614
After 3 months:  £11,956
After 6 months:  £14,295
After 1 year:    £20,440
After 2 years:   £41,783

1.5% per week. That's it. No home runs. No doubling accounts.
Just consistent, boring, disciplined gains.
```

## MODULE 4: RISK-REWARD RATIOS — THE TRUTH NOBODY TELLS YOU {#module-4}

### Why Risk-Reward Matters More Than Win Rate

Here's something that'll change how you think about trading forever.

You do NOT need a high win rate to make money. What you need is a favourable risk-reward ratio.

Let me prove it:

```
SCENARIO 1: High Win Rate, Bad RR
──────────────────────────────────────────
Win Rate: 70%
Risk per trade: £100
Reward per win: £100 (1:1 RR)

Per 100 trades:
70 wins × £100 = £7,000
30 losses × £100 = £3,000
NET PROFIT: £4,000

SCENARIO 2: Low Win Rate, Good RR
──────────────────────────────────────────
Win Rate: 40%
Risk per trade: £100
Reward per win: £300 (1:3 RR)

Per 100 trades:
40 wins × £300 = £12,000
60 losses × £100 = £6,000
NET PROFIT: £6,000

SCENARIO 2 MAKES MORE MONEY WITH A LOWER WIN RATE.
```

Read that again. 40% win rate with 1:3 RR beats 70% win rate with 1:1 RR.

This is the maths that separates professionals from amateurs.

### The Minimum Acceptable Risk-Reward

```
RULE: Never take a trade below 1:1.5 risk-reward.
IDEAL: Aim for 1:2 minimum on every trade.
BEST: 1:3+ when the setup is perfect.

WHY 1:2 IS THE SWEET SPOT:
──────────────────────────────────────────

At 1:2 RR, you only need a 34% win rate to break even.
At 1:2 RR with 50% win rate, you're solidly profitable.
At 1:2 RR with 60% win rate, you're crushing it.

Most decent strategies have 45-60% win rates.
So at 1:2, you're almost certainly making money.
```

### How to Measure Risk-Reward BEFORE Entering

Before you click "Buy" or "Sell," you must know:

```
STEP 1: Where is my stop loss? (Define the RISK in pips)
STEP 2: Where is my take profit? (Define the REWARD in pips)
STEP 3: Divide reward by risk. Is it 1:2 or better?

EXAMPLE:
Entry: 1.0900
Stop: 1.0875 (25 pips risk)
Target: 1.0960 (60 pips reward)
RR: 60 ÷ 25 = 1:2.4 ← TAKE THE TRADE

EXAMPLE 2:
Entry: 1.0900
Stop: 1.0860 (40 pips risk)
Target: 1.0940 (40 pips reward)
RR: 40 ÷ 40 = 1:1 ← SKIP IT. Not worth the risk.
```

### The Partial Take Profit Strategy

Some traders like to take partial profits along the way. Here's how to do it properly:

```
THE 50/25/25 MODEL:
──────────────────────────────────────────

Entry: 1.0900 | SL: 1.0875 | Full TP: 1.0960

- At 1:1 (1.0925): Take 50% off. Move SL to breakeven.
- At 1:2 (1.0950): Take another 25% off.
- At 1:3 (1.0975): Let final 25% run to full target.

BENEFITS:
- You lock in profit early (reduces stress)
- Breakeven stop means zero risk on remaining position
- Final portion can catch bigger moves
- Psychologically easier to manage

DOWNSIDE:
- Reduces your average win size
- Overall expectancy drops slightly vs. full target holds
```

### When to Let Trades Run

Sometimes the market gives you more than your target. How do you know when to hold?

```
LET IT RUN IF:
──────────────────────────────────────────
✓ Price is in a strong impulse (no pullback on M15)
✓ No major liquidity/resistance between you and the next level
✓ You've already taken partials and are playing with "free money"
✓ Higher timeframe structure supports continuation

TAKE YOUR TARGET IF:
──────────────────────────────────────────
✓ Price is approaching a major HTF level
✓ You see momentum slowing (smaller candles, wicks forming)
✓ It's end of session (London close, NY close)
✓ Major news event coming within 2 hours
```

### The Expectancy Formula — Know Your Edge

```
EXPECTANCY = (Win Rate × Average Win) - (Loss Rate × Average Loss)

EXAMPLE:
Win Rate: 55%
Average Win: £200 (1:2 RR)
Average Loss: £100

Expectancy = (0.55 × £200) - (0.45 × £100)
           = £110 - £45
           = £65 per trade

This means: On AVERAGE, every trade you take earns you £65.
Over 100 trades = £6,500 expected profit.

IF YOUR EXPECTANCY IS NEGATIVE — STOP TRADING THAT STRATEGY LIVE.
```

## MODULE 5: THE PSYCHOLOGY OF RISK — YOUR BRAIN VS. YOUR ACCOUNT {#module-5}

### Your Brain Is Wired to Lose Money

I'm not being dramatic. There's a Nobel Prize-winning theory that explains exactly why most people are terrible at managing risk. It's called Prospect Theory, and it proves something brutal:

**Humans feel the pain of losing £100 roughly 2.5 times more than the pleasure of gaining £100.**

This means your brain will make you do irrational things to AVOID losses — even when those things make your losses bigger.

### The 5 Psychological Traps

**Trap 1: Loss Aversion — Moving Your Stop Loss**

```
WHAT HAPPENS:
Trade goes against you. Stop loss is 5 pips away from being hit.
Your brain screams: "Just give it a bit more room!"
You move the stop. Trade goes further against you. Now you're down 3% instead of 1%.

THE FIX:
Your stop loss is a COMMITMENT. Once it's placed, it doesn't move.
If you can't handle watching a trade approach your stop — don't watch.
Set it and walk away.
```

**Trap 2: Disposition Effect — Cutting Winners Early**

```
WHAT HAPPENS:
You're up £150 on a trade. Target is £200. 
Your brain screams: "Take the profit! It might reverse!"
You close at £150. Price hits your original target 2 hours later.

THE FIX:
Set your TP at entry. Don't touch it.
If you MUST manage it — use partials (50/25/25 model from Module 4).
Never close a full position early out of fear.
```

**Trap 3: Sunk Cost Fallacy — Averaging Down**

```
WHAT HAPPENS:
You bought EURUSD at 1.0900. It's now at 1.0850. You're losing.
Your brain screams: "If I buy more here, my average price improves!"
You add to the position. Price drops to 1.0800. Now you've doubled your loss.

THE FIX:
NEVER average into a losing position. NEVER.
If your original analysis was right, the stop shouldn't have been hit.
If the stop gets hit, you were WRONG. Accept it. Move on.
```

**Trap 4: Gambler's Fallacy — "I'm Due a Win"**

```
WHAT HAPPENS:
You've lost 4 trades in a row. 
Your brain screams: "The next one HAS to be a winner!"
You size up. You take a mediocre setup. You lose again — bigger this time.

THE FIX:
Each trade is INDEPENDENT. Past results don't affect future probabilities.
After 4 losses, your job isn't to win — it's to STOP and reassess.
The 2-loss daily rule exists for exactly this reason.
```

**Trap 5: Overconfidence — After a Winning Streak**

```
WHAT HAPPENS:
You've won 5 trades in a row. You feel invincible.
Your brain screams: "I'm on fire! Let me size up!"
You risk 3% instead of 1%. The next trade loses. 
One loss wipes out 2 of your 5 wins.

THE FIX:
Winning streaks don't make you a better trader.
They mean your strategy is working IN CURRENT CONDITIONS.
Conditions change. Stay humble. Stay at 1%.
```

### The Emotional Audit — Before Every Trade

```
ASK YOURSELF:
──────────────────────────────────────────

1. Am I entering this trade based on my PLAN or my FEELINGS?
2. Am I trying to "make back" a loss? (If yes — DON'T TRADE)
3. Am I sizing up because I "feel good"? (If yes — DON'T SIZE UP)
4. Can I handle this trade losing and feel NOTHING? 
   (If no — you're risking too much)
5. Would I take this exact setup on a boring Tuesday with no emotions?
   (If no — skip it)
```

### Building Emotional Resilience

```
DAILY PRACTICES:
──────────────────────────────────────────

1. JOURNAL your emotions, not just your trades
   "I felt anxious before entering. I felt greedy when up 50 pips."
   
2. MEDITATE for 5-10 minutes before trading
   Not woo-woo stuff — just sitting quietly and breathing.
   It calms your amygdala (the panic part of your brain).

3. PHYSICAL EXERCISE before the session
   15-minute walk, push-ups, anything.
   Physical release reduces cortisol (stress hormone).

4. DETACH from money
   Think in % and R multiples, not pounds.
   "I lost 1R" feels different from "I lost £500."
   
5. ACCEPT that losses are tuition
   Every loss teaches you something — if you journal it.
   Reframe: "What did this cost me?" → "What did this teach me?"
```

## MODULE 6: ACCOUNT PRESERVATION STRATEGIES {#module-6}

### The First Rule of Trading: Don't Lose Your Account

Before you think about making money, think about NOT losing money. Preservation first. Growth second. Always.

### Strategy 1: The "Risk Budget" Approach

Think of your risk like a monthly budget. You only have a certain amount to "spend" on trades.

```
MONTHLY RISK BUDGET:
──────────────────────────────────────────

Account: £10,000
Monthly risk budget: 6% = £600
Risk per trade: 1% = £100

That means you can "afford" 6 full losing trades per month
before you hit your monthly circuit breaker.

6 losses in a month at 1% = STOP. Review. Reassess.
```

### Strategy 2: The Equity Curve Management

Track your equity curve (account balance over time). If it dips below a certain threshold, reduce activity.

```
EQUITY CURVE RULES:
──────────────────────────────────────────

Account at or above starting balance:
→ Trade normally (1% risk, 2-3 trades/day max)

Account 1-5% below starting balance:
→ Reduce to 0.75% risk, 1-2 trades/day max

Account 5-10% below starting balance:
→ Reduce to 0.5% risk, 1 trade/day max, A++ setups only

Account 10%+ below starting balance:
→ STOP. Go back to demo for 1 week minimum.
```

### Strategy 3: The "House Money" Effect

Once your account grows, protect your starting capital by mentally separating it:

```
EXAMPLE:
──────────────────────────────────────────

Started with: £10,000
Current balance: £12,500
"House money" (profits): £2,500
"My money" (starting capital): £10,000

RULE: If your account drops back to £10,000, 
you've "spent" all your profits. STOP and reassess.

This means your maximum acceptable drawdown from the peak 
is the amount of profit you've made — not your original capital.
```

### Strategy 4: Scaling In and Out

Instead of going all-in on one entry, scale into positions:

```
SCALING IN (3 entries):
──────────────────────────────────────────

Total intended risk: 1% (£100)
Split into 3 entries: 0.33% each (£33 per entry)

Entry 1: At the order block
Entry 2: At the FVG below (if price dips further)
Entry 3: At the next structural level

BENEFIT: Better average price if all entries trigger.
SAFETY: If only Entry 1 triggers and stops out, you only lost 0.33%.
```

### Strategy 5: Correlation Awareness

This is something most traders completely ignore. And it kills accounts.

```
HIGHLY CORRELATED PAIRS (move together):
──────────────────────────────────────────
EUR/USD and GBP/USD → ~80% correlation
AUD/USD and NZD/USD → ~85% correlation
USD/CHF and USD/JPY → ~70% correlation

WHAT THIS MEANS:
If you're long EUR/USD AND long GBP/USD at 1% each,
you DON'T have 2% diversified risk.
You have nearly 2% concentrated risk on "USD weakness."

If the dollar strengthens, BOTH trades lose simultaneously.

RULE: Maximum 2% total risk on correlated positions.
Treat correlated pairs as a SINGLE trade for risk purposes.
```

### Strategy 6: The "3 Strikes" Daily Rule

Simple. Effective. Life-saving.

```
Strike 1: First losing trade → Normal. Continue.
Strike 2: Second losing trade → Caution. Take a 30-min break.
Strike 3: Third losing trade → DONE for the day. No exceptions.

WHY IT WORKS:
- Prevents spiral trading (losses leading to bigger losses)
- Forces you to walk away before emotions take over
- Limits daily damage to 3% maximum (at 1% per trade)
- Tomorrow is always there. Today's market doesn't owe you anything.
```

```
   If yes → WHY? What triggered it? How do I prevent it?

3. CONSEQUENCE SYSTEM — if you break a rule:
   → Mandatory 1 day off trading (even if in profit)
   → Reduce risk to 0.5% for the next 3 trading days
   
4. VISUAL REMINDER — print your rules. Put them where you trade.
   Read them EVERY morning before opening a chart.

5. TRADING JOURNAL FLAG — in your journal, add a column:
   "Rules followed? YES / NO"
   Track your compliance rate. Aim for 95%+.
```
